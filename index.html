Alexandre Benoist

PhD student in number theory at the University of Luxembourg.

Welcome to my webpage ! I am a PhD student in mathematics at the University of Luxembourg, working under the supervision of Antonella Perucca. My interests mainly lie in number theory, and I am currently working on the arithmetic of abelian varieties and isogenies. During the first year of my PhD, I worked on the distribution of Elkies primes for reductions of abelian varieties and on two variants of a conjecture of Lang and Trotter on primitive points for reductions of elliptic curves.
